# MegaCSV logo guidelines

## 1. The logo

**Idea:** the blue whale, the largest animal on earth, in our cobalt. Its throat grooves are rows of data. Big and calm: the thing that swallows files Excel can't.

| Version | File | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal lockup (primary) | `svg/lockup-horizontal.svg` | Site header, documents, most places |
| Stacked lockup | `svg/lockup-stacked.svg` | Square spaces: social posts, slides, stickers |
| Whale only | `svg/whale.svg` | When the name is already nearby; illustrations |
| Wordmark only | `svg/wordmark.svg` | Very wide, short spaces |
| App icon | `svg/app-icon.svg` | App and PWA icon, social avatars |
| Favicon | `svg/favicon.svg` | Browser tabs and anything under 48 px |

The wordmark is Hanken Grotesk SemiBold converted to outlines (SIL Open Font License, free for logo use). Never retype it; use the files.

## 2. Clear space

Keep empty space of at least **one eye-to-snout distance** around the logo (about a quarter of the whale's height). It scales with the logo.

## 3. Minimum size

| Version | Screen | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal lockup | 120 px wide | 30 mm wide |
| Whale | 40 px wide | 12 mm wide |
| Below that | use `favicon.svg` (simplified whale on a cobalt tile) | use the app icon |

The full whale has grooves, a back fin and two flippers that blur below about 40 px. The favicon drawing drops them and tilts the whale to fill the square.

## 4. Colour

| Name | HEX | RGB | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cobalt | `#2449F5` | 36, 73, 245 | Whale body, app tile, primary buttons |
| Navy | `#0C1652` | 12, 22, 82 | Near flipper, eye |
| Periwinkle | `#6E88FA` | 110, 136, 250 | Far flipper |
| Tint | `#B9C6FF` | 185, 198, 255 | Throat grooves |
| Ink | `#17181A` | 23, 24, 26 | Wordmark on light |
| Ground | `#F3F4F2` | 243, 244, 242 | Page background |

Print: cobalt is outside what CMYK reproduces exactly. Ask the printer to match `#2449F5` on a Pantone swatch book (start from Pantone 2728 C) and approve a proof before printing.

**Approved pairs:**
- Full colour on white or ground.
- `whale-reversed.svg` (white whale) on cobalt.
- `lockup-horizontal-on-dark.svg` on dark backgrounds.
- `whale-one-color-*.svg` for single-ink jobs: embroidery, stamps, engraving.

## 5. Don'ts

- Don't stretch, squash, rotate or flip the whale. It always swims to the right, toward the name.
- Don't recolour it outside the palette, and don't use Excel green.
- Don't add shadows, outlines, gradients or glows.
- Don't rearrange or resize the whale and name inside a lockup.
- Don't put the full whale on busy photos; use the app icon instead.
- Don't use the full whale below 40 px; use the favicon drawing.

## 6. Web icons (already installed in `app/public/`)

`web/` holds the full set:
- `favicon.ico` (16/32/48) and `favicon.svg`
- `apple-touch-icon.png` (180, full bleed; iOS rounds the corners)
- `icon-192.png` and `icon-512.png`
- `icon-maskable-512.png` (Android adaptive icons; the whale sits inside the safe zone)
- `site.webmanifest`

`head-snippet.html` has the matching `<head>` tags.

## 7. Source and rebuilding

Everything is generated by `design/logo/kit.py` from the drawing in `design/logo/whale.py`. Change the drawing there and rerun:

```bash
python3 design/logo/kit.py
cp design/logo/kit/web/{favicon.ico,favicon.svg,apple-touch-icon.png,icon-192.png,icon-512.png,icon-maskable-512.png,site.webmanifest} app/public/
```

**Before launch:**
- Run a trademark search (USPTO, WIPO Global Brand Database, IP India).
- Do a reverse image search.
- Note that Docker also uses a whale. Ours is clearly different, but have the search cover it.

The presentation board (`presentation.html`, slides in `presentation/`) shows the logo in use. Its business card uses placeholder name and email.
