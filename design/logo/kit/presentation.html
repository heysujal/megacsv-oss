What we heard

The brief

A free browser tool for Excel users holding CSV files too big for Excel, often confidential and on locked-down work laptops. It should feel calm, capable and trustworthy, and be memorable enough that people tell colleagues.

calmbigfriendlytrustworthy

Distinct from grids, tables and arrows used by data tools; a character the brand can use everywhere; readable as a 16 px favicon.

Concept A

The blue whale

The largest animal on earth, in cobalt; its throat grooves are rows of data.

  • Big and calm: the thing that swallows files Excel can't
  • A character for loading screens, empty states, stickers and the 404 page
  • Simplified silhouette carries the favicon at 16 px
