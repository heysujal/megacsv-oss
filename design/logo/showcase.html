MegaCSV identity directions

Brand idea: MegaCSV is the biggest thing in the ocean of data, and it's calm. Each direction is shown the way people will meet it: on light, cobalt and dark, in the browser tab and site header, as an app icon at true sizes, and as a sticker.

1 · The blue whale

The largest animal on earth, in our cobalt. Its throat grooves are rows of data. Calm, big, friendly: the thing that swallows files Excel can't. A real character the brand can use everywhere: loading states, empty states, stickers, the 404 page.

Recommended. Most identity of the four; a mascot people remember and talk about.

4 · Halftone M

An M printed in halftone dots: one big file made of many small values. The dots give the brand a texture for backgrounds and loading animations.
