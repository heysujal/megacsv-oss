Questions

Is my file uploaded?

No. Your browser reads it on this computer. Turn off Wi-Fi and it still works.

How big can the file be?

Tens of gigabytes. The limit is your disk, not Excel.

Will it change my data?

No. Leading zeros, long numbers and dates stay exactly as they are in the file.
