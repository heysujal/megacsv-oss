Questions

Is my file uploaded?

No. Your browser reads it on this computer. Turn off Wi-Fi and it still works.

How big can the file be?

Tens of gigabytes. The limit is your disk, not Excel.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari. Chrome or Edge is best for very large files.

Will it change my data?

No. Values are shown exactly as they are in the file. Leading zeros and dates stay untouched.
