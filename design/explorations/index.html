MegaCSV: 8 design directions

Click one to try it. Each has a home page and the view after a file opens: click “Try a sample file”, or drop any CSV on the page. The round icon switches light and dark. Inside a file, type in “Find” to see matches highlighted. Left picture: home page (dark). Right picture: file open (light).

9 · Quiet office (5 + 6 combined)Home page from Quiet instrument: calm narrow column, one blue accent, with a small preview of the real app. The app uses Familiar office's layout (big buttons, formula bar, column letters, sheet tabs) in the same quiet colours, with the plain-sentence filter above the grid.
1 · MonochromeBlack and white with one orange accent, in Vercel/shadcn and rare-ui style. The live sample file sits under the headline. Export panel on the right. The safe, polished option.
2 · Departure boardSplit-flap letters flip into place. Files are listed like arrivals with a status lamp. Row counts flip on open. The loudest and most memorable; dark by default.
3 · Green-bar printoutOld green-and-white printer paper with sprocket holes, a nod to finance's big data dumps. The hero tears off at row 1,048,576: “Excel stops here.” Filters read as one sentence.
4 · Swiss posterA huge headline with a live row counter set inside it, in strict grid, black, white and red. Big row counts sit above the grid in the file view.
5 · Quiet instrumentCalm, one narrow column, very little decoration, one blue accent. Filters are written as a sentence (“Show rows where vendor contains Acme…”). Export sits in a dark bar at the bottom.
6 · Familiar officeFeels like home to Excel users: a ribbon of big buttons, a formula bar, column letters, and sheet tabs at the bottom (“Acme Q3 · 12,408 rows”). Teal, not Excel green. The most familiar.
7 · Cobalt counterThe whole page is bold cobalt blue. A giant rolling row counter, plus a ruler you drag to set your file size and see the rows and the time to open. Based on rare-ui's counter.
8 · Punch card“One row is one card.” A real punch card whose holes encode an actual row, stamping in when the page loads. Warm and playful, with pink, mint and periwinkle cards.
