**Verdict:** choose Gigasheet if you want a cloud spreadsheet for very large data, shared online, or its healthcare price data, and your files are allowed to leave your computer. Choose [MegaCSV](/app/) if the file is confidential or you just need to open, find, filter and export it now: it's free, needs no account, and never uploads your file.

| | MegaCSV | Gigasheet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | Free | Free to start; paid Premium Individual plan; enterprise plans on request |
| Account | None | Sign-up required |
| Where the file goes | Stays on your computer | Uploaded to Gigasheet's servers |
| Rows | No limit (tested to 192 million, 20 GB) | Up to 1 billion in a single sheet (advertised) |
| Waiting before you can look | First rows in about 0.1 s | Upload time first |
| What you can do | Find, filter, sort, Summarize, remove duplicates, export | "Analyze and transform" in a spreadsheet interface |
| Export | CSV or Excel, split to fit, free | CSV download; Excel export not mentioned |
| Healthcare price data | No | Yes, its main product today |

Gigasheet details are from its home page, pricing page and Big Spreadsheet Viewer page, checked November 2, 2026. Its prices aren't published there; check gigasheet.com/pricing.

## Where Gigasheet is better

Data that already lives in the cloud or needs to be shared there, and its licensed healthcare datasets (negotiated rates and provider data).

## Where MegaCSV is better

Privacy (nothing is uploaded, so it suits client and regulated data), speed to first look (no upload wait), no account or payment, and free Excel exports of any size, split to fit.
